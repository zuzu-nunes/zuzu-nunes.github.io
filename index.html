About

I have a B.S. in Cybersecurity and have spent the last three years solving real problems for real users as an on-site help desk technician, including managing a fleet of 600 Macs through Mosyle MDM. Before that, I worked two years on my college's IT team running backups and restores with Commvault, which is where I learned that a backup only matters if you can actually restore it.

I'm now focused on cloud security: studying for the AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner exam and building hands-on projects that combine AWS, infrastructure as code, and the backup/recovery experience I already have.
